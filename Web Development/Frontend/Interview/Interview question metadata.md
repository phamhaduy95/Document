This document defines the standard structure for an interview assessment record. Each record must include the following fields:
- **Prompt:** A brief summary of the problem statement or scenario presented to the candidate.
- **Assessment Domain:** The specific evaluation area (e.g., Live Coding, Theoretical Knowledge, Problem Solving).
- **difficulty**: three levels ( basic, intermediate and advanced )
- **answer**: it is common to have multiple answers for one single question. => For this case, we should provide at least 2 distinct answers. If candidate manages to give novel and correct answer, give the candidate a bonus point.
- **Negative Signals:** Common incorrect responses or "anti-patterns" that indicate a fundamental knowledge gap, such as answers that demonstrate guessing rather than actual comprehension.
- rating score: The number that evaluates the candidate's overall skill from 1 to 5
