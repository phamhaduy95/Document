
**AWS QuickSight** is a fully managed business intelligence (BI) service that supports **data visualization** and **dashboard** creation. It can connect directly to **Amazon S3** and **Amazon RDS** for PostgreSQL as data sources, making it ideal for querying and visualizing data from the company’s data lake.

QuickSight allows the creation of datasets that combine data from multiple sources (S3 and RDS), enabling unified reporting and analysis.

It supports interactive dashboards that can be shared with users or groups, with fine-grained access control to meet the requirement of limiting access.
