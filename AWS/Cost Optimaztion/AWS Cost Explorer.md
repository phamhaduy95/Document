#### Introduction

AWS Cost Explorer is a built-in tool within AWS Billing and Cost Management that helps you visualize, understand, and manage your AWS costs and usage over time.

**Feature**:
- **Cost and Usage Reports**: Customizable ==graphs and tables== to drill down into daily or monthly costs by filters like service, account, or usage type.
- **Forecasting**: AI-powered predictions of future spend based on historical patterns.
