Characteristics of good software design:
	- **cohesion**: put unrelated things far part and ensure related things close together.
	- **modularity**: breaking software into smaller independent modules each of which has specific function.
	- **abstraction and depth:** hide inner implementation and dependencies. The actual logic underneath should be deep.
