> in hindsight: to imply that your current understand is different from your past understanding.
> look back the problem with new perspective.
> They did not succeed in developing a good management software for the company. **In hindsight,** we know why they failed.  (we look back at the past event with current knowledge and understanding)
> 
> I were worried when I could not go to my dream university. **In hindsight**, my alternative choice was the best thing happened as it led me to a very successful career I and having today. 

> [!note] **Meaningful**
> The word "**meaningful**" is an adjective used to describe something that has significance, importance, or a profound purpose.
> We found his teaching career to be incredibly **meaningful**. ( significant positive impact and highly important)
> She have made **meaningful** contribution to the organization.
