The goal of note-taking is to capture ideas whenever they pop into your head and keep it for further use. They are not meant to be kept forever as they only serve as reference materials for more important future documentation and are likely to be deleted upon completion. 
write as much as possible, even just a small idea come up in your head. Organize those intermediate thoughts as note that can be put for future use. 


divide your notes into three different categories:
1. fleeting note: temporary note, the reminder of information, which can be threw away in 2 or 3 days. It can be written in arbitrary way.
2. permanent note: The note is meant to be kept for long times, it contain necessary information and good enough to be reused in more longer writing content such as book or news
3. project note: a collections of note per project:

 ideal structure for project note folder
	- TODO lists
	- draft
	- reminders
	- outlines
	- the collections of relating literatures
